# SilentSuite roadmap

### Native iOS app
Planned · [#52](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/52)

Sync with the built-in Calendar, Contacts and Reminders apps on iPhone and iPad; iOS isn't supported yet.

### Links between notes, tasks, events and contacts
Planned · [#51](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/51)

Connect a note, task or event to the people and other items it relates to.

### Private event invitations
Planned · [#574](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/574)

Invite another SilentSuite user to a single event, end-to-end encrypted, without sharing a whole calendar.

### Other CalDAV and CardDAV providers
Planned · [#46](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/46)

Bring calendars and address books from accounts such as Google, iCloud or Nextcloud into encrypted SilentSuite collections.

### Share your calendars out over CalDAV
Planned · [#769](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/769)

An opt-in way to publish chosen calendars to people and services outside SilentSuite, readable by them and not end-to-end encrypted.

### Editing notes on Android
Planned · [#793](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/pull/793)

Write and edit notes in the Android app; an early prototype exists, but it isn't ready.

### Two-factor sign-in
Planned · [#145](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/145)

A second sign-in step with an authenticator app or a hardware security key.

### Birthdays calendar
In progress · [#738](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/738) · [#916](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/pull/916)

An optional calendar that shows the birthdays saved in your contacts.

### Better web app on phones
In progress · [#295](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/issues/295)

Easier navigation and layouts that fit small screens for calendars, contacts and tasks in a phone browser.

### Umbrel app
In progress · [getumbrel/umbrel-apps#6286](https://github.com/getumbrel/umbrel-apps/pull/6286)

A SilentSuite package for umbrelOS, submitted to the Umbrel App Store and not available there yet.

### Notes, experimental
Completed · 2026-09-14 · [v0.5.7-beta](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/releases/tag/v0.5.7-beta) · [v0.5.11-beta](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/releases/tag/v0.5.11-beta)

End-to-end encrypted Markdown notes in the web app, off by default; Android can read them since 2026-10-04, but not edit them.

### Encrypted sharing
Completed · 2026-06-21 · [v0.3.0-beta](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/releases/tag/v0.3.0-beta)

Share calendars, address books and task lists with other SilentSuite users, end-to-end encrypted.

### Multiple calendars, address books and task lists
Completed · 2026-05-25 · [v0.2.0-beta](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/releases/tag/v0.2.0-beta)

Keep several calendars, address books and task lists, each synced as its own encrypted collection.

### First public beta
Completed · 2026-05-03 · [v0.1.0-beta](https://github.com/silent-suite/silentsuite/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta)

The web app, the Android app, the desktop Bridge and self-hosting shipped together as the first public beta.
